The Ivonne Van Cleef Orquesta

The Ivonne Van Cleef Orquesta is an instrumental project exploring desert rock, dark western and rural psychedelia. Through loops, drones and analog textures, the project builds cinematic sonic landscapes shaped by repetition and guided improvisation. Each performance unfolds as an open structure in constant transformation.

Cruce entre desert rock, dark western, psicodelia rural y música cinematográfica.

Paisajes eléctricos, tensión ritual y narrativa sonora.

The Ivonne Van Cleef Orquesta is an instrumental project exploring desert rock, dark western and rural psychedelia.

Through loops, drones and analog textures, the project builds cinematic sonic landscapes shaped by repetition and guided improvisation.

Each performance unfolds as an open structure in constant transformation.
